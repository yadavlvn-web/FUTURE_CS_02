# Phishing Email Detection – Comparison Report

## Project

Future Interns – Cyber Security Internship

## Objective

To identify phishing indicators and compare two fictional email samples.

## Sample 1: Bank Account Warning

**Classification:** Suspected Phishing

### Indicators

* Urgent language pressures the recipient to act quickly.
* The threat of account suspension may frighten the recipient.
* The email asks the recipient to verify an account using a link.
* The sender's identity has not been independently verified.

### Potential Risk

The recipient may visit a fake login page and disclose sensitive information.

### Recommendation

Do not click the link. Verify bank alerts through the official bank app or website.

## Sample 2: Interview Schedule

**Classification:** No obvious phishing indicators in the provided text.

### Observations

* The email provides interview scheduling information.
* It does not request passwords or banking details.
* No link is included in the sample.
* The sender's identity has not been independently verified.

### Recommendation

If uncertain, verify the interview details through the company's official recruitment channel.

## Conclusion

The first sample contains several phishing indicators. The second sample has no obvious phishing indicators in the provided text. However, text alone cannot prove that an email is genuine.

## Scope and Limitations

Both samples are fictional and created for educational practice. No real emails, links, accounts, or systems were tested.
