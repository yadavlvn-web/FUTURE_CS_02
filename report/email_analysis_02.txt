## Email Classification

No obvious phishing indicators in the provided text.

## Observations

1. The email provides interview scheduling information.
2. It does not request passwords or banking details.
3. It does not contain a link in the provided sample.
4. The sender's identity has not been independently verified.

## Potential Risk

Even an ordinary-looking email may be fraudulent if the sender is impersonating a real company or recruiter.

## Recommended Actions

* Verify the sender's email address.
* Confirm interview details through the company's official recruitment channel if uncertain.
* Do not share sensitive personal information without verification.
